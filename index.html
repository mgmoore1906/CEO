Labor market analysis

Ideas that can manifest true student workforce readiness.

Independent regional analysis using TEA Labor Market Information, Texas Workforce Commission data, and O*NET occupational profiles — mapping which Greater Houston occupations are actually growing, what they pay, which credentials gate entry, and where a high school program of study can put a student on the right side of that line. It's the same evidence base a Comprehensive Local Needs Assessment runs on, and I've been doing it voluntarily.

This is the gap a CTE department exists to close. HB 8 realigned community college funding to credentials that carry labor market value; CCMR accountability asks districts for the same. The districts that win the next decade will be the ones whose programs of study were chosen from data rather than inherited from a course catalog.

Cohort for Economic Opportunity

What I built when no one assigned it to me.

CEO is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit I founded to give talented students — especially those who have been systematically overlooked — the economic navigation capital to move with intention through high-value career pathways.

It connects leadership, workforce readiness, entrepreneurship, and social emotional learning in one intentional experience. The through-line: ambition is evenly distributed and navigation isn't.
